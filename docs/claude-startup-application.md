**One-liner:**
OrgMind is an open-source AI productivity platform for whole organizations: shared knowledge,
department-level workflow agents, and permission-aware AI, not just a personal assistant per employee.

**Problem:**
Companies buy AI seats for individuals, but the gains stay personal. Knowledge stays in silos,
meeting outcomes get lost, managers can't see where time goes, and sensitive documents leak
across departments when people paste them into chat tools.

**Solution:**
A self-hostable platform where every document, user and workflow belongs to a department.
Claude answers questions only from documents the user may see, converts meetings into owned tasks,
writes department reports for leadership, and shows org-level analytics on AI use and bottlenecks.

**How we use Claude:**
Claude powers grounded Q&A with citations over company knowledge, structured extraction
(meeting notes → tasks JSON), and report generation. Next: MCP connectors (Gmail, Drive, Slack, Trello)
and multi-step agents for approvals and onboarding.

**Why open source:**
Companies can audit how their data is handled and self-host. Revenue later comes from a managed
cloud, SSO/audit features and support.

**What credits enable:**
Running a hosted demo, evaluating Claude models on long-document retrieval, and onboarding
3–5 pilot companies over the next 6 months.
